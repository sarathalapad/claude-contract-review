# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. This is a synthetic contract written for software demonstration only.
> All parties, addresses and terms are invented. It is not legal advice and must not be used as a template.

Contract type: nda
Party A: Harbourline Retail Group (fictional)
Party B: Pellucid Design Studio LLP (fictional)
Effective date: 15 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to exchange Confidential Information to evaluate a possible store redesign project (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the Purpose, and protect it with at least reasonable care.

## 3. Residuals

Either party may use Residuals for any purpose, where Residuals means information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel.

## 4. Term

The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for one (1) year after the date of disclosure.

## 5. Return or Destruction

On written request, each party shall promptly return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information.

## 6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
