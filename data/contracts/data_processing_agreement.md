# Data Processing Agreement

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. This is a synthetic contract written for software demonstration only.
> All parties, addresses and terms are invented. It is not legal advice and must not be used as a template.

Contract type: dpa
Processor: Cloudmere Software Ltd (fictional)
Controller: Harbourline Retail Group (fictional)
Effective date: 1 March 2026

## 1. Scope

This Data Processing Agreement applies to Personal Data that Processor processes on behalf of Controller when providing the Services under the Subscription Agreement.

## 2. Processing Instructions

Processor shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Controller, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law.

## 3. Data Location

Personal Data will be hosted in data centres located in the European Union and the United States.

## 4. Sub-processors

Processor may engage sub-processors without prior notice to Controller. Processor remains liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors.

## 5. Security and Breach Notification

Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data. Processor shall notify Controller of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within seven (7) days of becoming aware of it.

## 6. Audit

Processor shall make available to Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and shall allow for audits, including inspections, conducted by Controller or an auditor mandated by Controller once per year.

## 7. Return and Deletion

On termination of the Services, Processor shall return or delete all Personal Data at the choice of Controller within thirty (30) days.

## 8. Governing Law

This Data Processing Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland.
