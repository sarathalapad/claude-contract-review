# Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. This is a synthetic contract written for software demonstration only.
> All parties, addresses and terms are invented. It is not legal advice and must not be used as a template.

Contract type: saas
Provider: Cloudmere Software Ltd (fictional)
Customer: Harbourline Retail Group (fictional)
Effective date: 1 March 2026

## 1. Definitions

"Services" means the hosted inventory forecasting platform described in Order Form 001. "Customer Data" means all data uploaded to the Services by or on behalf of Customer. "Fees" means the subscription fees set out in the Order Form.

## 2. Services

Provider shall make the Services available to Customer in accordance with the service levels in Schedule 2. Provider shall maintain industry-standard technical and organisational security measures.

## 3. Term and Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all invoices within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 2% per month until paid. Provider may increase the Fees at its sole discretion upon thirty (30) days written notice.

## 5. Customer Data

Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use Customer Data to improve its products and services. Customer Data may be stored and processed in any country where Provider or its subcontractors operate.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations survive for five (5) years after termination.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the claim. Customer's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice. On termination Provider shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the courts of Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction.
